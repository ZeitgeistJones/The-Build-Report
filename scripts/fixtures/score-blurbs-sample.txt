Integration with leftclaw.services (documented in README) enforces job pickup and completion via onchain contracts (leftclaw-services handles payment routing). --- clawd-containers / User funds, risk, and safety posture ---
No user funds held in clawd-containers itself — it is a job-execution orchestrator, not a wallet or payment contract.
